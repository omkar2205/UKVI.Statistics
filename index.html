Data Guide

Definitions, source and scope.

Data source

UK Home Office immigration system statistics. Open official data tables.

Sponsored Study Visa

This dashboard uses main applicant sponsored study applications and outcomes only.

Rates

Grant rate = Issued ÷ (Issued + Refused). Refusal rate = Refused ÷ (Issued + Refused).

Comparison caution

Rate comparisons show the decision base. Results with fewer than 30 decisions are flagged as a small sample.

Interpretation

Applications and outcomes are period-level indicators. They are not matched applicant-level records.
